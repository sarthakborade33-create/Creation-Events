Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries about booking, design consultations, and event pricing in Mumbai.

How far in advance should we book Creation Events?

For weddings and major corporate galas, we recommend booking us at least 4 to 6 months in advance. For birthday parties and baby showers, 1 to 2 months notice is ideal. However, feel free to contact us for last-minute availability.

Do you operate outside of Mumbai?

Yes, while our primary base of operations is Mumbai (covering South Mumbai, Suburbs, Navi Mumbai, and Thane), we also undertake destination weddings and luxury corporate events across Goa, Pune, Lonavala, and Alibaug.

Can we customize our decoration themes?

Absolutely! We specialize in tailored designs. During our initial creative consultation, we will map out your vision, colors, and references to construct a completely customized 3D layout of the event set prior to execution.

Do you handle sound systems and entertainment acts?

Yes, we offer comprehensive event management. This includes premium line array sound systems, DJ bookings, live bands, customized stage lighting, professional EMCEEs, and dynamic entry setups.
