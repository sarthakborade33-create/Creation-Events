About Creation Events

Creation Events is a Mumbai-based event management and entertainment company with over 10 years of experience in delivering exceptional event experiences across India.

We specialize in B2B and B2C event solutions, offering everything from corporate events and brand activations to weddings, exhibitions, school events, kids' carnivals, DIY workshops, game stalls, and food stalls.

With 1,000+ successfully completed events, we focus on creating engaging, interactive, and memorable experiences tailored to every client's vision. Our experienced team manages every detail—from planning to execution—ensuring a smooth and stress-free event.

Whether you're organizing a corporate gathering, wedding celebration, mall activation, or private party, Creation Events provides complete entertainment and event solutions under one roof.
